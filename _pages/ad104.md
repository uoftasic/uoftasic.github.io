## AD104-M: Magic

**[Go to AD104-M](https://uoftasic.com/ad104-m/)**

Draw an inverter in Magic until it passes DRC and LVS, build a second one from
Magic's device generator, measure what its parasitics cost in delay, then place
and route four standard cells by hand.

Take this version: it's the one that's open now.

## AD104-K: KLayout

**Not open yet.** This version uses KLayout, with Magic's device generators.

Draw an inverter in KLayout until it passes DRC and LVS, build a second one
from generated transistors, measure what its parasitics cost in delay, then
place and route four standard cells by hand.

The course is moving to KLayout, and this is the version that will stay.

The other courses are listed at [edu.uoftasic.com](https://edu.uoftasic.com/).
