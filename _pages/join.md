## General membership

Any student can join as a member and work on whichever initiatives interest them.

1. Fill out the [membership form]({{ site.organisation.membership_form }})
2. Join the [UofT ASIC Discord]({{ site.organisation.discord }})
3. Introduce yourself and say which teams you want to work with

## Directorship

We recruit directors for subteams such as Webmaster and Socials on a rolling basis.
To apply, email **[{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }})** with your portfolio
and why you are interested. We will follow up with interview details.

## Proposing a project

The Design Kompetition accepts project proposals on a rolling basis. A proposal
covers milestones, development structure, director involvement, and technical
documentation referencing existing designs. Once selected, you onboard onto the
project and work toward a tapeout milestone — see
[Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) for what that produces.
